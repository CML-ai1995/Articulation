/R/ Sound Placement Guide

1. Bunched /R/ Position

Pull the tongue body back into a high "mountain" shape. The sides of the tongue press firmly against the top back molars.

2. Retroflex /R/ Position

Curl the tip of the tongue up and back towards the roof of the mouth without touching it.

Key Reminders: Keep lips slightly squared, turn on vocal cords (voice on), and pull tongue back tight!
